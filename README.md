Two-line explanation of an everyday thing (a toaster) for a 10-year-old, entered in the make.fun competition "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run".

## The entry

> A toaster is a tiny lift that carries your bread down into a glowing hot basement and brings it back up brown and crunchy.
>
> The lift explains the up-and-down trip and the browning, but it leaves out the glowing wires that do the actual cooking — and real lifts never toast their passengers.

Line 1 explains the toaster through a single vivid analogy (a tiny lift). Line 2 says what the analogy maps (the up-and-down trip, the browning) and what it leaves out (the glowing wires that do the cooking).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (290 characters, 2 lines) and verifies it against the competition gate: UTF-8 text, 1–400 characters, not only whitespace, exactly two non-blank lines.
